# ClientFlow — Product Requirements (MVP)

ClientFlow is a simple, lightweight CRM for small businesses (2–20 employees) to track the core customer workflow: Lead → Follow-up → Deal → Customer.

## MVP Scope

- **Authentication:** Sign up, sign in, sign out, and session management.
- **Workspaces:** Multi-tenant isolation for organizations.
- **Team Members:** Role-based access control (Owner, Admin, Member).
- **Leads:** Create, edit, assign, and track status/sources.
- **Contacts:** Customer record management reusable across the CRM.
- **Deals:** Pipeline tracking (New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Won/Lost).
- **Tasks:** Follow-up and task assignment with due dates.
- **Dashboard:** Actionable metrics (total leads, open deals, overdue tasks).

**Philosophy:** Simple over feature-heavy. Useful over impressive. Secure by default.
